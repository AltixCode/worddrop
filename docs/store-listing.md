# Store listing & submission

Nothing here may claim anything the build does not do (AGENTS.md rule 1; Apple
2.3.1 / 4.2, Google Play Deceptive Behavior). Enforcement is account-level.

## Identity

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |

| Bundle id / package | `com.altixcode.worddrop` |

## Copy

**Subtitle (App Store, 30)**: `Daily film word puzzle`
**Short description (Play, 80)**: `One film word a day. Six guesses. Share your grid, keep your streak.`

**Keywords (App Store, 100, comma-separated, no spaces, no repetition of the
name or subtitle, no competitor trademarks):**
`word game,guess,letter,movie,cinema,brain,daily,streak,puzzle,vocabulary,trivia,offline`

> Do **not** use "Wordle" anywhere — it is a New York Times trademark.

**Description — first three lines carry the pitch:**

```
A new film-themed word every day, the same one for everyone in the world.
Six guesses, a streak worth protecting, and a spoiler-free grid to share.
One optional payment removes every ad forever — no subscription, no account.

Guess the day's word from the category, with a clue if you need one after three
tries. Letters you place correctly turn green; letters in the wrong place turn
yellow (or blue and orange if you switch on high-contrast colours).

• A new puzzle at midnight, worldwide
• Plays offline once the day's puzzle is on your device
• Streak, win rate and guess distribution, kept on your phone
• Share your result as an emoji grid that spoils nothing
• Optional daily reminder at a time you choose
• 14 languages, with full right-to-left layout in Arabic and Persian

WordDrop is free because of ads. A single one-time purchase removes them for
good and opens the archive of every past puzzle. There is no subscription and
there is no account to create.
```

## Assets

- **Icon**: `assets/icon.png`, 1024×1024, generated by `scripts/make-icons.mjs`.
- **Screenshots**: capture from a real simulator/emulator with
  `.maestro/screenshots.yaml` — never a marketing render that does not match the
  build. Required: 6.7" iPhone and 13" iPad; phone and 7"/10" tablet for Play.
  First two carry the value proposition: the board mid-game with colour
  feedback, then the shareable result grid.

## Localization of the listing

The app ships fourteen locales; the stores do not accept the same set.

- **App Store Connect has no Persian/Farsi storefront locale.** List in the
  other thirteen. Do not remove `fa` from the app to match the store.
- **Google Play supports `fa-IR`**, so the Play listing carries all fourteen.
- Keywords are per-market research, never a translation of the English field.

## Privacy labels — must match the build

The app collects nothing itself, but it is **not** a "no data collected" app:

| Disclosed | Why |
| --- | --- |
| RevenueCat: anonymous app user id, store receipt, country | Required to sell and restore the lifetime unlock |
| Google AdMob: device identifiers, coarse usage data, IP | Serving ads; used for tracking only if the player consents through the UMP form |
| Nothing else | Puzzle progress, streaks and statistics never leave the device |

The UMP consent form runs before the first ad request, and ads default to
**non-personalised** until the player says otherwise. iOS additionally shows the
App Tracking Transparency prompt (`NSUserTrackingUsageDescription` in
`app.json`).
